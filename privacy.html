Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

I only ask for the information I need to answer your enquiry and teach you to drive. I don’t sell it, I don’t send you marketing, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Who I am

This website belongs to Ahmad, an independent automatic driving instructor in Liverpool (“I”, “me”). I am responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach me using the details at the bottom of this page.

What I collect

  • When you request a call back: your name, phone number and postcode, the type of lesson you’re interested in, and anything you choose to tell me in the message box.
  • When you call, message me on WhatsApp or email me: your contact details and whatever you include in your message.
  • If you become my pupil: the details I need to teach you safely and help with your test, such as your provisional licence details, lesson times, notes on your progress and records of payments.

Please don’t send me sensitive information, such as health details, unless it matters for your lessons. If it does, I’ll only use it to keep your lessons safe.

How I use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and arrange a first lesson
  • to plan, run and keep track of your lessons
  • to help you book your driving test, if you ask me to
  • to keep the business and tax records the law requires

The law lets me do this because you’ve asked me about lessons or booked them (taking steps towards, or carrying out, our agreement), because I have to keep certain records by law, and because it is in my legitimate interest to run my business properly.

Who can see it

Only me. I never sell your details or share them for marketing. Your information may pass through the services I use to run my business, such as my phone, email and WhatsApp providers, and the company that hosts this website and delivers form messages to me. If you ask me to book or change a test for you, I share the necessary details with the DVSA.

How long I keep it

If an enquiry doesn’t lead to lessons, I delete your details within 12 months. If you become my pupil, I keep your lesson and payment records for as long as I need them, and financial records for up to six years, as UK tax rules require.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • show you the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information, unless I have to keep it by law
  • stop or limit how I use it
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere

Just get in touch and I’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and this website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind. To display its fonts, your browser loads them from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I use your information, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
