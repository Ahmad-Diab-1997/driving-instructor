Driving lessons

Lessons that start from where you are now.

Whether you've never sat in the driver's seat or you passed years ago and want your confidence back, every course is planned around you.

Beginner lessons

No experience needed

Your first lessons cover the basics properly: getting comfortable in the car, moving off and stopping, steering and simple junctions. We start on quiet roads and only move on when you're ready.

  • Just your provisional licence
  • Clear goals for every lesson
  • Theory test guidance

Intensive courses

For learners who want momentum

Several hours of driving each week, over a shorter period. Good if you learn best with momentum, or have a job or university place waiting.

We'll be honest about realistic timescales. They depend on your experience and on test availability at your local centre.

Refresher lessons

For drivers who already hold a licence

Not driven for a while, moved to the city, or nervous about motorways and big roundabouts? A few focused lessons can make a real difference.

Test preparation

For learners with a test date coming up

Mock tests on local routes, independent driving with a sat nav, manoeuvres and the show me, tell me questions, so the 40 minutes on the day feel familiar.

How you'll learn

Automatic lessons only.

Every lesson is in an automatic car. With no clutch to control and no gears to change, you can put all your attention on the road, other traffic and your observations.

A

Why automatic?

Fewer things to manage at once makes learning calmer, especially in Liverpool's stop-start city traffic.

  • Less to think about while you learn
  • Smooth, relaxed driving in queues and on hills
  • Lessons in a quiet, smooth hybrid car

Nervous learners

Nervous? You’re in calm hands.

Many of my learners start after a bad experience, a long break or years of putting it off. We go at your pace, and you won’t face busy roads until you’re ready.

  • Explained until it clicks.Clear, step-by-step explanations at your pace, repeated as often as you need.
  • Lessons around your life.Early mornings, evenings and weekends, planned around school, shifts and childcare wherever possible.
  • Progress you can see.Each lesson builds on the last, so you always know what we’re working on and why.

How your first lesson works

  1. We talk first

    A quick call to understand any experience you have, what worries you and when you're free.

  2. A calm first drive

    I’ll pick you up from home, school, college or work, and we start on quiet roads, so you can get to know the car before going anywhere busy.

  3. A plan you agree with

    At the end, we talk about how it went and what comes next. There's no pressure to book a block of lessons.

Local roads

Learn on the roads you'll be tested on.

Liverpool’s roads can be busy. We practise every kind of road together, step by step, so nothing is new on your test day.

  • Terraced streetsParked cars on both sides, tight gaps and knowing when to wait.
  • Bus lanes and one-way systemsReading signs and road markings early, especially near the city centre.
  • Multi-lane roundaboutsChoosing the right lane, signalling clearly and keeping your position.
  • Dual carriagewaysJoining at speed, changing lanes and leaving safely.
